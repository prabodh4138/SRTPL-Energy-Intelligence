# Phase 3.3 KPI Catalog
Solar: daily/monthly generation, savings framework, contribution, zero-generation days, availability.
DG: generation, runtime, kWh/hour, PF, voltage/current imbalance, fuel efficiency and cost framework.
EB: kWh consumption, kVAh consumption, PF, kWh/kVAh; MD retained pending engineering confirmation.
Transformer: voltage/current/PF/frequency/temperature/imbalance; loss/efficiency disabled pending boundary confirmation.
UPS: input/output/bypass electrical values, battery values, availability and anomaly metrics.
Data quality: received, valid/invalid, completeness, duplicates, exception rate, source coverage.
All KPI results must retain calculation version and source-reading traceability.
